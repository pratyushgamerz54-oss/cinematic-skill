## Real footage (public domain)

- SR-71 take-off at Edwards Air Force Base (NASA Armstrong Flight Research Center), Wikimedia Commons
  `File:SR-71 Takeoff at Edwards Air Force Base.webm`. Shown as the A-12's successor, labelled "SR-71 (A-12 परिवार)".
- ER-2 pilot from suit-up to take-off, GEMx B-roll (NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, SVS14728), Wikimedia Commons.
  The ER-2 is NASA's civil version of the U-2; labelled "NASA ER-2 (U-2 का नागरिक रूप)".

## Map and terrain data

- 3D terrain of Groom Lake and its surroundings: AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen terrarium; USGS 3DEP/NED and SRTM, public
  domain / open data). Relief colours, lakebeds and all overlays are drawn in code; the base, runway, boundary, ridge
  and zone positions are approximate and labelled "प्रतीकात्मक 3D मॉडल" / "लगभग".
- 3D globes: land outlines from Natural Earth via world-atlas (public domain / ISC); no borders drawn.

## 3D models and motion graphics (own work, made in code)

- three.js: the terrain map (`assets/3d/terrain.html`), the U-2, A-12 and HAVE BLUE models (simple geometry, not to
  exact plans, `aircraft.html`), and Bob Lazar's S-4 claim (`s4.html`, labelled "दावा, सबूत नहीं").
- HyperFrames (`hf/`): YouTube intro (welcome, topic, go), the opening question, quote cards (Haines 1997, AARO 2024),
  the closing question and the reel's opening question.

## Voice, music, sound

- Narration: the creator's own recording with the ElevenLabs voice "Viraj" (film and reel recorded separately).
- Score: dark drone generated with ElevenLabs sound generation (made for पिछवाड़े के साँप), looped and cross-faded.
- Sound effects: synthesised in code (build_sfx.py).
