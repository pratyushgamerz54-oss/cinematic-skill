## Recreations drawn in code

- "THE DAILY RECORD · CURSE OF THE PHARAOH": an illustrative newspaper page typeset in code (labelled RECREATION); it
  stands for the April 1923 press coverage, not a specific paper.
- Cards (Abydos list, family tree, causes of death) are typeset in code from the sources in `script.json` fact_notes.

## 3D, maps and motion graphics (own work, made in code)

- 3D globes (three.js): g_luxor, g_carnarvon, g_verdict. Land outlines: Natural Earth via world-atlas (public domain / ISC).
- 2D map of Egypt (engine GeoMap, look "intel"): same Natural Earth data, borderless land.
- Illustrative 3D models (three.js, labelled "प्रतीकात्मक 3D मॉडल"): the sealed door and the KV62 cutaway (after the
  published plan, not survey-accurate), the three nested coffins, the Valley of the Kings with the KV9 debris, and a
  symbolic scan diagram (not the mummy; no human remains are shown).
- HyperFrames motion graphics: the YouTube intro (welcome, topic, go), the opening question, the "wonderful things"
  quote card, the closing question, the reel's opening question.

## Voice, music, sound

- Narration: the creator's own recording with the ElevenLabs voice "Viraj" (film and reel recorded separately).
- Score: dark drone generated with ElevenLabs sound generation (made for पिछवाड़े के साँप), looped and cross-faded.
- Sound effects: synthesised in code (build_sfx.py).
